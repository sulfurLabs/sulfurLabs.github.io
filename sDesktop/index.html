sDesktop - the simpler modern

sDesktop is a small replacement for normal Xorg/Wayland desktops
it's intended to be for small hobbyOS projects which are still too small for Xorg or even Wayland
the most interesting part is PyritUI which is the form of stylesheet for the deskop, it tells it
where to draw, how to draw and how various things should work, its syntax is intended to be
simple enough for a 10 year old to understand. The desktop itself will have simple bilinear scaling for
modern graphics and already has a very simple damage marking for good and fast graphics.
